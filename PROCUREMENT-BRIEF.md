# The Companion Room: procurement brief

Two integrated wash/dry units have a verified manufacturer starting price of $24,500. The complete room requires a local construction quotation. The current premium allowance of $121,200 to $218,400 is authored and unquoted; it gives the team a starting budget conversation.

Prepared October 29, 2026. Draft request for quotation; not issued. No supplier or contractor has been contacted for this package.

## Price both room options

The development brief is one approximately 14-story building in East Lansing with 300 to 500 apartments, mostly studios. Final floor plans, services and the room allocation are unresolved. Price the two options on the same stated base-building assumptions.

| Option | Illustrative interior envelope | Base program | Expansion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compact | 20 ft x 18 ft; 360 sq ft | Two integrated wash/dry bays, one quick paw rinse, finishing counter, clean/used towel separation and locked service storage | Expansion separately evaluated |
| Premium | 24 ft x 22 ft; 528 sq ft | Same essential washing program with more circulation and storage | Reserve third-bay space; itemize future provisions |

These are planning envelopes. Bidders should identify fit issues, service-access constraints and any required area changes. The project architect establishes the final layout and engineering requirements.

## Equipment to price

Use Evolution mini as the reference for included resident washing and drying. The current manufacturer price starts at $12,250 per unit. Its package lists an integrated dryer, metered products, microbubble technology and filtration. The published warranty is ten years on the frame/tub and three years on parts, conditioned on use of the manufacturer's supplied soaps and products. [Manufacturer product page](https://evolutiondogwash.com/models-and-pricing/models/mini-series/)

The linked March 2024 manual documents factory Free Mode. Its nominal dimensions are 66 in wide x 54 in high x 24 in deep, with a recommended 7 ft x 7 ft operating footprint. Obtain a current submittal covering door/step projections, reach and entry, removable service panels, connection details and noise data. The manual's 1.5-inch sanitary-connection text and 2-inch drawing label need supplier clarification. [Manufacturer manual](https://evolutiondogwash.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/mini-Owners-Manual-final-March-2024-R-4.1-.pdf)

Quote two units, one paw-rinse fixture and complete compatible accessories. Itemize delivery, freight, rigging, startup, tax, training, commercial warranty coverage, local service, lead time and quote validity. Confirm supply cost per use and replenishment terms. Confirm that the ordered free-use configuration needs no resident payment.

An All Paws TUB-Amenity alternate should include its rinse/dryer controls and hair straining, with ready-to-use soap supplied by management. A current public price was not verified. [All Paws model](https://allpawspetwash.com/apw-option-c.html)

For a component-price benchmark, Groomer's Best lists its 58-inch ADA tub at $3,246 and a three-year warranty. The package includes the tub, legs, ramp, door and levelers; plumbing kit and grates are additional. Its $6,492 pair price covers tubs; a comparable integrated scope would also require separately priced drying and dispensing equipment. Obtain the full commercial warranty terms. [Manufacturer listing](https://www.groomersbest.com/product/ada-walk-through-bathing-tub/)

## Construction quotation scope

Provide separate compact and premium totals, with labor, material and assumptions visible. Break out room preparation and partitions; waterproofing and durable wet-area finishes; plumbing and drainage; electrical and lighting; ventilation and acoustic design; cabinetry and accessories; permits, design coordination, testing and commissioning. Allocate shared building services and fire-protection work explicitly.

The City lists building and trade-permit processes. The architect and contractors should confirm required submissions, inspections and fees for the actual building. [East Lansing Building Information](https://www.cityofeastlansing.com/648/Building-Information)

Price premium third-bay provisions separately from purchase and installation of a third unit. Also separate optional retail fit-out. State exclusions, contingency, schedule dependencies and the work supplied by others. Request written confirmation of equipment service access after all finishes are installed.

## Resolve before commitment

Confirm the room's location, pet-entry route, available services and actual floor plate. Then compare complete bids, review operations with property management and reconcile the allowance. No East Lansing contractor quotation is present in this project record. The compact option remains unpriced; a square-foot reduction alone cannot establish its cost. No rent uplift or return on investment is assumed.
