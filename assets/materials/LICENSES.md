# Material sources

Poly Haven CC0 assets, downloaded 2026-10-29.

- Marble 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01
- Oak Veneer 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01
- License: https://polyhaven.com/license

The 1K JPEG diffuse, roughness and OpenGL normal maps are retained locally, with an additional 4K marble diffuse map. Large stone slabs sample a clean region of the texture through the material shader to avoid baked grout lines. Download MD5 hashes matched the Poly Haven API. Stone colors are warmed in the render material for this concept.
